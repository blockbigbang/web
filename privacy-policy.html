Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Block Big Bang mobile game (hereby referred to as "Application") created as a free, single-player game. This service is provided by the developer "AS IS".

100% Ad-Free Experience

Block Big Bang does not display any third-party advertisements. We do not integrate ad networks, behavioral tracking software, or advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID). Your gameplay will never be interrupted by banner ads, interstitials, or rewarded marketing videos.

No Data Collection or Transmission

The Application does not collect, record, track, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII), precise location data, contact lists, or sensitive user information.

The Application operates completely on your local device without sending personal analytics to external backend servers.

Local Storage & Offline Gameplay

All core settings—such as sound volume, vibration preferences, and highest scores achieved—are saved solely inside your device's private local application storage. This data never leaves your device and can be erased at any time by clearing the application storage or uninstalling the game.

Third-Party Services

Because the Application is distributed through the Google Play Store, foundational operating platform services provided by Google may manage installation and crash reports per Google's global terms:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

We do not use AdMob or any external commercial telemetry networks.

Children's Privacy Protection

The Application does not collect, request, or solicit information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, Block Big Bang is safe for users of all ages and compliant with global digital child privacy principles, including COPPA.

Security

We value your privacy. Because no user data is transferred over the internet or saved to cloud servers, your information is protected against remote unauthorized access, interception, or disclosure.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect software improvements or policy adjustments. Any revisions will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
